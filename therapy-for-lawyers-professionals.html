Therapy for Lawyers & Professionals

For thoughtful, capable people who’ve built a career around performance — and need somewhere to set it down.

I work with lawyers and other high-achieving professionals who are navigating the pressures of demanding careers, including burnout, life transitions, and challenges in their relationships or sense of self. Many of the people I see are thoughtful and capable individuals who have built careers around responsibility, achievement, and relentless output, but who may also be feeling overwhelmed, disconnected, or unsure about what comes next.

I also work with couples where one or both partners are lawyers or high-achieving professionals. Demanding careers can put real strain on a relationship: long hours, unpredictable schedules, the need to always be reachable, the difficulty of leaving work stress at the door, or partners whose ambitions pull them in different directions. Couples therapy offers a space to slow down, understand the patterns you've fallen into, and reconnect with each other outside the pressures of work.

Before becoming a psychotherapist, I practiced law at a large law firm and in government. My experience in the legal profession gives me a firsthand understanding of the intensity of high-pressure work environments and the unique challenges that can accompany them.

My approach is psychodynamic and relational, with a focus on helping people better understand themselves, their patterns, and how those patterns show up both at work and in their relationships. Therapy offers a space to step back from the pace of professional life, reflect more deeply, and approach challenges with greater clarity and flexibility.

We begin with an initial consultation, where we'll talk about what's bringing you in and what you're hoping for. This is also a chance for you to get a sense of how I work and whether it feels like a good fit.

If you would like to learn more about how I work or to set up a free 15-minute consultation, please feel free to contact me.
