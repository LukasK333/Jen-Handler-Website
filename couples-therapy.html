Clinical Services

Couples Therapy

A relational approach to understanding what’s gotten in the way — and reconnecting.

I work with many couples who are navigating conflict, distance, or simply a sense that something between them isn’t working the way it used to. Some couples come in during a crisis; others come in because they want to strengthen a relationship that’s already fairly solid.

With couples, I draw on both my relational psychoanalytic training and the concepts of emotionally focused therapy (EFT), which is considered the gold standard of couples therapy. Together, we’ll focus on the emotions that are contributing to disconnection in your relationship, and work toward promoting empathic connection between you and your partner. You can read more about the thinking behind this on my Approach page.

This process often means slowing down enough to notice the patterns that repeat in an argument, or in a silence — not just what was said, but what each of you were feeling and needing underneath it.

Getting Started

We begin with an initial consultation, where we’ll talk about what’s bringing you in and what you’re hoping for. This is also a chance for you and your partner to get a sense of how I work and whether it feels like a good fit.
