Clinical Services

Clinical Supervision

Private supervision for therapists deepening their skills within a relational psychoanalytic framework.

I also provide private clinical supervision to therapists looking to deepen their therapeutic skills. I work in a contemporary relational psychoanalytic framework and make use of that framework to help you explore any themes and issues that may be coming up for you in the treatment of your patients.

I am currently a supervisor and the Co-Director of Supervision for the Four-Year Adult Training Program at the National Institute for the Psychotherapies, for candidates completing advanced training in psychoanalysis. You can read more about my background and training on my About Me page.

If you would like to learn more about my approach to supervision, or would like to know more about working with me as a private supervisor, please contact me.
