Who I Am

A former lawyer, now a psychoanalyst

I work with individuals and couples — including lawyers and other high-achieving professionals, as well as creative individuals and students — offering an LGBTQIA+ affirming approach, with particular experience in this area.

Before becoming a psychotherapist, I worked as a lawyer in private practice and in government. I draw on that experience in my work with people navigating high-pressure environments and career transitions.

JD, LCSW · Board Member & Supervisor, National Institute for the Psychotherapies

Read more about me

The Work

“I’ll try to fully understand the world as you experience it — and help you change what isn’t working.”

My approach is psychodynamic and relational: rooted in the belief that the relationship we build together is what creates room for real, lasting change. I work within a contemporary psychoanalytic framework, drawing on emotionally focused therapy (EFT) principles when I work with couples.
