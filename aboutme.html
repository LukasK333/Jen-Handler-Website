About Me

A psychotherapist and psychoanalyst in Manhattan — and, before that, a lawyer.

In my practice, I see individuals and couples, including lawyers and other high-achieving professionals, as well as creative individuals and students. I offer an LGBTQIA+ affirming approach, with particular experience in this area.

Prior to becoming a psychotherapist, I worked as a lawyer in private practice and in government. I draw on that experience in my work with people navigating high-pressure environments and career transitions — work I now focus on directly in my practice with lawyers and other professionals.

I serve on the Board of Directors at the National Institute for the Psychotherapies (NIP), where I also teach and supervise. You can read more about that work on my clinical supervision page.

My clinical orientation is psychodynamic and relational — you can read more about how that shapes the actual work we’d do together on my Approach page.
