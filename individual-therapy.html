Clinical Services

Individual Therapy

A space to understand yourself more fully — and to change what isn’t working.

I work with individuals navigating anxiety, depression, self-doubt, and uncertainty about the direction their life is heading — as well as people who are simply hoping to understand more about themselves. Many of the people I see are students, creative professionals, and high-achieving professionals, including lawyers, who are used to holding a lot together and are looking for a space where they don’t have to.

My approach is psychodynamic and relational. Rather than focusing only on symptoms, we’ll work to understand the patterns underneath them — in your relationships, your work, and your sense of yourself — and how those patterns took shape. Over time, this kind of understanding tends to open up real choices you didn’t feel you had before. You can read more about this on my Approach page.

I offer an LGBTQIA+ affirming approach, with particular experience in this area.

I strongly prefer to work with people a minimum of once a week, and ideally two or three times a week. I find that meeting more frequently opens up richer avenues of exploration and produces faster results, but we’ll find an approach together that fits your goals and needs.

Getting Started

We begin with an initial consultation, where we’ll talk about what’s bringing you in and what you’re hoping for. This is also a chance for you to get a sense of how I work and whether it feels like a good fit.
